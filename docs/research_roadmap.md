# Scope of a future source-inference study

The released methods note is complete for its restricted fixed-template scope.
The following items remain before making new astrophysical parameter claims:

1. Fit a physical network likelihood over intrinsic and extrinsic parameters.
   Include consistent antenna factors, delays and physical waveform phases.
2. Specify finite priors, their parameterisation and Jacobians. Match the
   conventions of any official posterior chosen for comparison.
3. Use posterior sampling with independent runs, effective sample counts,
   autocorrelation or nested-sampling diagnostics as appropriate, and evidence
   uncertainty where model selection is claimed.
4. Perform injections spanning the inference region, including mismatched
   waveform families and real off-source noise. Assess recovery and coverage.
5. Treat calibration, PSD uncertainty and nonstationary transients explicitly.
6. Compare joint released posterior samples, not only marginal medians.
7. Define the original scientific result and obtain independent scientific review.

No outstanding item is described as complete in the current release. Converged
sampling and finite priors are not retroactively implied by the conditional
amplitude tests or analytic profile likelihood.
